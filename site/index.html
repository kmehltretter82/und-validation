THE APPROACH01 / 03

A note on the name

A little more
constructive.

Tertium non datur is the Latin name for the law of excluded middle. We borrow the name to explore a different question: what can we build without assuming it for every proposition?

Here, proofs are checked by Lean and certified results are audited for their dependencies. The library is independent of Mathlib, and designed to grow one construction at a time.
